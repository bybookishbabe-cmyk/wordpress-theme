reader type badges

the chaos reader

maximum spice, maximum plot complications, very little concern for emotional safety.

key tropes
5/5 spice or anything that says burn it down and make it romantic.
the dark romance girlie

danger, obsession, morally gray choices, and devotion with teeth.

key tropes
dark romance, dangerous moods, stalker romance, mafia romance, and villain-coded love interests.
the fantasy girlie

magic systems, fated stakes, crowns, monsters, and dramatic yearning.

key tropes
romantasy, paranormal romance, fated mates, and fantasy-leaning shelves.
the jersey chaser

athletes, rivalry, teammates, locker-room confidence, and softness after the game.

key tropes
sports romance, hockey romance, baseball romance, or athlete-with-heart energy.
the slow burn girlie

glances, restraint, almost-confessions, and payoff that takes its sweet time.

key tropes
slow burn moods, yearning-forward books, and readers who want tension before touch.
the tension addict

key tropes
enemies to lovers, forced proximity, rivals, and antagonistic chemistry.
the fake dating fanatic

key tropes
fake dating, one bed, forced proximity, and relationship-of-convenience setups.
the sweet romance devotee

comfort, tenderness, caretaking, and low-spice softness that still knows how to ache.

key tropes
cozy moods, lower spice levels, friends to lovers, small towns, and healing romance.
the romance reader

key tropes
